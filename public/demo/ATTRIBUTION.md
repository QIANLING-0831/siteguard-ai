# Demo image attribution

These files are used only as local, replaceable demo evidence.

- `construction-workers-tools.jpg`: “Construction hard workers with their tools”, U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, public domain. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Construction_hard_workers_with_their_tools.jpg
- `ironworkers.jpg`: “Construction Workers”, Paul Keheler, CC BY 2.0. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Construction_Workers.jpg

The annotations in the seeded database are workflow demonstrations, not verified model-accuracy claims.

The following synthetic construction scenes were generated with OpenAI ImageGen for this local workflow prototype: `site-floor.png`, `underground-mep.png`, `facade-scaffold.png`, and `rebar-yard.png`. They are explicitly labeled as synthetic demo evidence in the product and must not be used as accuracy-validation data.
